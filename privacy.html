Privacy Policy

Application: rclone-latex

Purpose

This application is used exclusively by its owner to synchronize LaTeX project files with Google Drive using rclone.

Google Drive Access

The application requests Google Drive access to read, upload, update, and manage files as directed by its owner.

Data Storage

Files remain on the owner's local computer and Google Drive. Authentication credentials are stored locally by rclone.

Data Sharing

This application does not intentionally share, sell, or transfer Google user data to third parties.

Access Revocation

The owner can revoke access through Google Account security settings.
